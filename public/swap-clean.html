Swap steps
  1. Connect wallet.
  2. Enter amount and click estimate.
  3. If approval says not enough, click approve BSC-USD.
  4. First Rabby confirmation: approve the exact BSC-USD (USDT) amount shown for PancakeSwap Router. This does not buy CIGO yet.
  5. Wait for the approval transaction to complete.
  6. Return here and click estimate again to verify approval.
  7. When approval shows the entered amount as approved, click swap.
  8. Second Rabby confirmation: swap the approved BSC-USD (USDT) amount for CIGO.
  9. After swap completes, approval resets. Click estimate before another swap.
Why two Rabby confirmations? A new exact-approval swap uses two separate blockchain transactions: first an approval, then the actual swap. If approval is already verified, only the swap confirmation is needed.
