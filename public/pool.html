LP notes

liquidity steps

  1. Connect wallet. Rabby should connect on BNB Chain. The button should change to a connected wallet address and wallet balances should appear below the buttons.
  2. Estimate amounts. Enter one field only: either CIGO or BSC-USD. The estimate button fills the other field from the live pool ratio. This step does not ask Rabby for a signature.
  3. Approve exact CIGO. Rabby should show a token approval for CIGO, the exact CIGO amount shown on this page, and spender 0x10ED43...56024E / PancakeSwap. Cancel if the token, amount, or spender is wrong.
  4. Approve exact BSC-USD. Rabby should show a token approval for BSC-USD / USDT, the exact BSC-USD amount shown on this page, and the same PancakeSwap router spender. Cancel if it shows CIGO again, unlimited approval, or a different spender.
  5. Add liquidity. Only after both exact approvals are complete, Rabby should show an add-liquidity transaction to PancakeSwap. Review both token amounts, slippage/minimums, gas, and LP result before signing. CIGO transfer tax may reduce the amount of CIGO that actually reaches the pool. This is not staking and no reward program is active. Start tiny.
Wallet confirmation is final. Do not sign if Rabby does not match the numbered step you clicked.
Liquidity note: Providing CIGO / BSC-USD liquidity helps create a working market with lower slippage and clearer price discovery. LP providers may earn pool trading fees, but they also accept pool exposure, price movement, impermanent loss, and inventory risk.

Adding liquidity is not a swap, staking, or fixed-yield deposit. CIGO may apply transfer tax when sent into the pool, so the approved or sent amount may be higher than the amount that reaches the pool. LP tokens represent your share of the pool only. No CIGO staking or reward program is active. Estimate first, approve exact amounts only, review Rabby carefully, and start with a tiny test.
