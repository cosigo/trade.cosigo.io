CIGO swap guide

Use this with the main CIGO swap/trade page.

open trade page ↗
1
Connect walletUse Rabby on BNB Smart Chain. Connecting only lets the page read your wallet.
2
Choose directionSelect the token you are sending and the token you want to receive.
3
Enter amountStart small. Check your CIGO / BSC-USD balance before continuing.
4
Get quoteReview estimated output, tax notes, slippage, route, and price impact.
5
Approve exact token only if askedRabby should show the token, exact amount, and PancakeSwap Router v2 spender 0x10ED43...56024E. Cancel wrong chain, wrong token, unlimited approval, or unfamiliar spender.
6
Execute swapRabby should show a PancakeSwap transaction. Review minimum received, gas, and price impact before signing.
7
Confirm resultAfter success, check wallet balance and BscScan. Reset before starting another swap.
Wallet confirmation is final. CIGO may include buy/sell tax, and PancakeSwap has its own pool fee.

Discount Desk guide

Use this when creating a reviewed request instead of relying on an instant wallet-only swap.

open discount desk page ↗
1
Prepare walletUse the correct wallet and BNB Smart Chain. Confirm your receiving address before submitting.
2
Select from / to tokensChoose CIGO, BSC-USD, or COSIGO according to the request you want reviewed.
3
Enter amountStart small if new. Review quote policy, caps, and any displayed limits.
4
Review request previewCheck amount, direction, wallet address, settlement notes, and expected output.
5
Submit requestSubmitting creates a reviewable request. It is not the same as sending funds.
6
Wait for reviewed instructionsDo not send funds until the request is reviewed and settlement instructions are visible.
7
Complete only after reviewFollow the reviewed instructions exactly and keep the request record for confirmation.
Do not send tokens or funds to any address unless that address appears inside the reviewed request flow.

Liquidity pool guide

Use this with the CIGO / BSC-USD pool tool. Adding liquidity is not staking and is not a fixed yield program.

open pool page ↗
1
Connect walletUse Rabby on BNB Smart Chain. Confirm you are on the correct wallet before estimating.
2
Estimate amountsEnter one side and let the tool estimate the matching CIGO / BSC-USD amount.
3
Review tax and pool notesCIGO transfer tax may reduce what reaches the pool. Review slippage and estimated deposit amounts.
4
Approve exact CIGORabby should show exact CIGO approval to PancakeSwap Router v2. Cancel unlimited approval or wrong spender.
5
Approve exact BSC-USDRabby should show exact BSC-USD / USDT approval to PancakeSwap Router v2.
6
Add liquidityReview final Rabby transaction before signing. Confirm amounts, gas, router, and chain.
7
Confirm LP tokensAfter success, confirm LP tokens and BscScan transaction. LP tokens represent pool share only.
Liquidity has risk: price movement, impermanent loss, slippage, taxes, pool imbalance, and failed/changed wallet confirmations.
